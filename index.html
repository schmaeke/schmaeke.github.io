Jan Niklas Schmäke

Computational Mechanics · Hochschule Düsseldorf

Mechanical engineer by training, working in computational mechanics research at Hochschule Düsseldorf — mostly on unfitted finite element methods, multi-level hp refinement and immersed boundaries, and the software that goes with them.

Projects

  • Unfitted.jl (Julia): unfitted multi-level hp refinement on Cartesian grids with finite-cell immersed-boundary integration.
  • FoamGen.jl (Julia): open-cell foam geometries for finite element and immersed-boundary tests.
  • Oritone (theme): a color theme and scientific colormaps for editors, terminals and visualization, after Okabe & Ito.
  • Oritone LaTeX (LaTeX): document classes and a Beamer theme for theses, manuscripts, CVs and slides.
